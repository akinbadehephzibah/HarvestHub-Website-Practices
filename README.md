# 🌾 Harvest Hub

**A smarter way to connect farmers, harvests, and storage.**

Harvest Hub is a web-based platform designed to address one of the major challenges affecting farmers and agricultural communities: **post-harvest losses caused by inadequate storage and difficulty accessing suitable storage facilities.**

The platform provides a simple way for farmers to discover available storage options, understand their storage needs, and connect with suitable facilities before their harvest is lost or damaged.

## 🌱 About Harvest Hub

Harvest Hub is a concept created to make agricultural storage easier to discover and understand.

For many farmers, producing a successful harvest is only part of the journey. Without proper storage, harvested crops can become damaged, spoiled, or difficult to sell at the right time.

Harvest Hub focuses on the stage **after harvesting**, helping farmers find information about storage options and make better decisions about where and how their produce can be stored.

The platform is designed with simplicity in mind, especially for users who may access it primarily through their phones.

---

## ⚠️ The Problem

Farmers can face several challenges after harvesting:

- Limited access to suitable storage facilities.
- Difficulty finding available storage spaces.
- Lack of clear information about storage facilities.
- Poor storage conditions that can affect crop quality.
- Difficulty comparing different storage options.
- Limited awareness of proper post-harvest storage practices.
- Loss of income when crops spoil before they can be sold.
- Difficulty connecting farmers with storage providers.

These challenges can contribute to significant **post-harvest losses** and reduce the economic value of agricultural production.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Harvest Hub provides a centralized digital platform where users can explore storage-related information and discover suitable storage options.

Instead of relying entirely on word-of-mouth or physically searching for facilities, farmers can use the platform to:

1. Understand their storage needs.
2. Explore available storage options.
3. View important facility information.
4. Compare suitable options.
5. Learn about proper storage practices.
6. Connect with storage providers.

The goal is to make the storage process **simpler, clearer, and more accessible.**

## 🔄 How It Works

### Step 1 — Tell Us What You Need

The user provides basic information about their harvest and storage requirements.

### Step 2 — Explore Storage Options

Harvest Hub presents relevant storage options based on the user's requirements.

### Step 3 — Review the Details

The user can examine information about a facility before making a decision.

### Step 4 — Compare Options

Users can consider factors such as location, capacity, storage type, and cost.

### Step 5 — Connect

The user can proceed to contact or request the selected storage provider.

### Step 6 — Store With Confidence

The farmer gets access to a storage solution that better matches their needs.
