THE POST-HARVEST CHALLENGE

The work doesn't end when the crop leaves the farm.

Growing a crop requires planning, labour, resources, patience and time. But after harvesting, farmers still need to protect what they have produced. This is where post-harvest storage becomes important.

The storage gap

Imagine a farmer has successfully harvested 200 bags of maize. The harvest is ready. But then the farmer asks: where can I store it?

The farmer may need to know: which facility is nearby? Does it accept maize? Is there enough space? How much does it cost? Is the facility currently available? What are the storage conditions? Who operates the facility? How can the provider be contacted?

Common challenges

Storage may exist. But can farmers find it?

The platform is not claiming to create storage facilities. Instead, it creates a digital connection between available storage and farmers looking for it — farmer, search, compare, contact.

Trust starts with better information.

Finding storage is one thing. Knowing what you're getting is another. Harvest Hub aims to provide clear information that helps farmers ask the right questions before making arrangements.

What does verified mean?

A Verified label can indicate that the provider's submitted information has passed the platform's defined verification process — reviewing provider identity, facility information, location, contact details, and supporting documents where required.

VERIFIED

Provider information has completed the platform's verification process.

UNDER REVIEW

Information has been submitted but verification is still ongoing.

UNVERIFIED

The listing has not completed verification.

Transparency

Every listing should make important information easy to understand.

  • Who? The storage provider.
  • Where? The facility location.
  • What? Supported crops.
  • How much? Available capacity.
  • How much does it cost? Pricing information where supplied.
  • Is it available? Current availability status.
Verification does not mean "never ask questions." Farmers should still confirm current availability, current pricing, storage conditions, additional charges, terms of storage, and payment arrangements. Harvest Hub helps you discover and connect — always confirm important details before committing your harvest.

THE HARVEST HUB GUIDE

A simple guide to finding suitable storage.

Before searching

  • Crop type — Know what you need to store.
  • Quantity — Estimate how much you have.
  • Location — Decide where you want the storage facility to be.
  • Duration — Know approximately how long you expect to store your crops.
  • Budget — Have an idea of what you can afford.

When choosing storage

  • Location — How accessible is the facility?
  • Capacity — Can it accommodate your quantity?
  • Crop compatibility — Does the facility support your crop?
  • Price — What does the storage cost?
  • Verification — What is the provider's verification status?
  • Communication — Can you easily contact the provider?
